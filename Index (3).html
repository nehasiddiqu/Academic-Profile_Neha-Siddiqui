03 / Technical research profile

Explainable AI for introductory programming education

Doctoral topic: Explainable AI framework for early identification and intervention of learning difficulties in introductory programming. Institution: Symbiosis International University. Status: PhD in progress (2025–present).

Research purpose

Investigate how explainable, data-informed approaches might help educators identify patterns of difficulty in introductory programming and design timely, meaningful learning support. The aim is to keep educators and learners able to understand, question and act on model outputs.

The following technical design is a proposed research direction based on the doctoral topic in the supplied CV. It is not a statement that specific datasets, models, experiments, ethics approvals or findings already exist.

Research questions to investigate

  • Which programming-learning indicators can support useful, early identification of difficulties without introducing unnecessary surveillance?
  • How might transparent model explanations assist teachers in understanding the factors associated with predicted learning needs?
  • How do predictive utility, fairness, privacy and pedagogical actionability trade off across possible approaches?
  • What types of educator-designed interventions could be evaluated following a risk signal?

Proposed technical workflow

01 · Research design & ethical data specification

Define the educational context, intended outcomes, consent and governance requirements. Potential indicators for investigation include assessment performance, concept-level errors, code submission patterns, formative feedback and task completion history, where lawfully available and appropriate.

02 · Data quality & feature engineering

Evaluate missing data, label quality, imbalance, leakage, sampling bias and cohort differences. Candidate features may include repeated syntax errors, debugging attempts, concept mastery signals and changes in formative performance. Feature selection would need to prioritise educational relevance and minimisation.

03 · Candidate modelling approaches

Compare interpretable baselines such as logistic regression and decision trees with possible ensemble models such as random forests or gradient boosting, subject to dataset suitability. Any more complex sequence modelling should be justified by sufficient longitudinal data and a clear educational benefit.

04 · Explainability & human review

Explore feature importance, local explanations, SHAP or LIME where appropriate, and counterfactual reasoning. Assess explanation stability, faithfulness and usefulness to teachers. Predictions should support professional judgement, not automated labelling or high-stakes decisions.

05 · Evaluation & educational relevance

Plan leakage-safe validation, suitable baselines, precision/recall, F1, calibration, error analysis and subgroup fairness assessment. If feasible and approved, examine whether explanations improve educator decisions and whether interventions support learning, rather than treating predictive accuracy as the sole outcome.

06 · Responsible implementation

Consider access controls, data minimisation, secure retention, documentation, bias monitoring and transparent communication to learners. Investigate appropriate human-in-the-loop processes and safeguards against stigmatisation.

My approach combines explicit conceptual foundations with hands-on projects, regular formative assessment, differentiated feedback and responsible use of AI. I aim to create inclusive learning experiences that build technical confidence and independent thinking.
